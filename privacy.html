Privacy Policy

Passage Planner · Effective 20 October 2026

Summary

Passage Planner is a planning aid for ship officers. It has no user accounts and no analytics, advertising or tracking. The developer does not collect, receive or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

Data stored on your device

  • Passage plans, routes, waypoint notes, voyage draft plans, ship particulars, hydrostatic tables, calculator inputs and settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device only.
  • Files you save (plan files, RTZ routes, backups) are written only where you choose, using your device's own save / share dialog.
  • You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Location

If you tap a GPS button, the app asks for permission to use your device's location, while the app is in use only. The position is used on the device to show the ship on the chart and to calculate sunrise / sunset. If you tap GPS under Weather at a position, that position is sent to Open-Meteo only to fetch the forecast for that place. Your location is never sent to the developer, never stored on a server and never used in the background.

Files you open

When you import a route (RTZ), a hydrostatic table (Excel / CSV), a NAVTEX log or a backup, you pick the file with the system file picker. The file is read on the device only.

Online services the app contacts

When you are online, the app downloads public information directly from these services. Like any website, they receive your device's IP address and the details of the request. No personal data from the app is included.

These services have their own privacy policies. The app does not send them your name, contact details or the ship's identity, and it sends your position only when you ask for the weather at your GPS position.

Children

The app is a professional tool for adult seafarers and is not directed at children.

Security

All online requests the app makes use encrypted HTTPS connections. Data stored on the device is protected by your device's own security (screen lock and encryption).
